Face Attendance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how the Face Attendance app handles information. In short: attendance data stays on the phone, and the app publisher does not receive it.

1. Who uses the app

The app is for teachers and school or college staff to record class attendance. It is not meant to be used by children themselves. The school or college that uses the app decides what data is entered, and is responsible for getting permission from students and parents or guardians where the law requires it.

2. Information the app handles

  • Class names, subject names, student names and attendance records (date, lecture, and Present, Late, Leave or Absent).
  • Face data. When a student is enrolled, the app turns the face into a list of 128 numbers (a face template) and keeps a small thumbnail picture of the face. This is biometric data.
  • Camera images while scanning or enrolling. They are processed live on the phone and are not saved, except for the small thumbnail.
  • Photos you choose when adding many students at once. They are processed on the phone and are not uploaded.
  • App settings such as theme and attendance rules, and the app PIN (kept only as a one-way hash).

3. Where it is stored

All of the above is stored only inside the app on this phone. It is not sent to the publisher or to any cloud service. Face recognition runs fully on the phone and works without internet. The app's storage is private to the app, but the app does not separately encrypt it. Please use a screen lock on the phone and the app PIN, and keep backup files safe.

4. When the internet is used

  • Premium purchase: payments are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. They receive purchase information and the school code.
  • Premium school code: the app contacts the publisher's licence server with the school code, a random phone ID and the time, to check that Premium is active and to count how many phones use the code. No student data is sent.

The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

5. Sharing and exports

When you share a class, make a backup, or export a report, the app creates a file and you choose where to send it (for example WhatsApp or email). Class and backup files contain student names, face templates and thumbnails. Share them only with trusted staff of your school. The publisher cannot see these files.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you delete it. You can edit or delete a student, delete a class, or use Settings, App lock, backup and data, Delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app. Backup files you made yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

7. Accuracy

Face recognition is not 100% accurate. Teachers should check the list and correct any mistakes before relying on it.

8. Requests and contact

Students and parents should ask their school to see, correct or delete their data, because the school holds it. For licence data (school code and random phone ID) or any question about this policy, contact: attendenceface@gmail.com

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here in the app.
